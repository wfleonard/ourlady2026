# Novena Social Campaign

Three social pushes tied to the Guadalupe Novena — one to announce/invite, one on the day it starts, one on the day it closes. All drive to `/novena` (and a soft link to `/gifts`).

Small campaign by design — the Novena is a devotional resource, not a product. Three posts per platform × four platforms = 12 posts.

**Blog URL:** https://primosmaternos.com/novena
**Image to attach on IG/FB:** any of the `/blog/*.jpg` heroes; the Juan-Diego one works well.

## Posting notes

- **Timing:** send Nov 28 mid-morning Eastern (day after Thanksgiving, low competition). Dec 3 and Dec 11 send 8–10 AM Eastern so subscribers see them at the start of the devotional day.
- **Don't stack:** if you're also running the Holiday Retail email campaign that same week, keep the Novena social separate from the retail email pushes — different intent.
- **Instagram:** IG doesn't allow clickable links in captions. Rotate `/novena` into your bio for the two-week window (Nov 28–Dec 12).
- **Facebook link preview:** URL on its own last line pulls the site's default preview image.
- **Reply-to expectation:** the Novena is a devotional post; expect prayer requests in comments. Answer them personally when you can.
