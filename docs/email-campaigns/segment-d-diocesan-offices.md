# Segment D — Diocesan Offices

Bulk B2B campaign targeting U.S. diocesan chanceries and their departmental offices (Hispanic ministry, faith formation, superintendents of Catholic schools, communications). Different persona than Segment A/B/C — decision-maker at scale, longer sales cycle, quotes rather than one-click checkout.

**Send window:** any time, but ideal is **October–February** — dioceses typically set annual budgets in the first quarter of the calendar year, and the Jubilee Year (through Oct 12, 2027) is a fresh, timely hook.

**Cadence:** three emails, spaced 7 days apart. Diocesan offices are slower to reply than parishes — leave more room between sends.

---

## Roles to target (in order)

1. **Director of Hispanic Ministry** or **Multicultural Affairs** (best fit for a Guadalupe-anchored initiative)
2. **Director of Faith Formation / Catechesis** (owns parish and school religious ed)
3. **Superintendent of Catholic Schools** (if pitching the school system rollout)
4. **Chancellor** (formal channel; slower but decisive)
5. **Bishop's secretary** (only if you know the bishop personally or by reputation)
6. **Director of Communications** (last resort — will forward, but doesn't decide)

## Notes on operational readiness

The /dioceses page and the emails above assume:

- **You can fulfill 100+ canvas orders within 3–4 weeks.** If lead time is longer, adjust both the page and the emails.
- **You accept Net 30 POs from diocesan offices.** If credit-check first, note that in your quote response.
- **You have letterhead invoicing ready.** Diocesan accounting requires a formal invoice; a Stripe receipt alone won't clear a PO.
- **Volume pricing is real and defensible.** The page shows tiers (1–4, 5–24, 25–99, 100+) without dollar amounts — quotes are custom. Have a working discount schedule internally before the first quote request lands.

Flag if any of the above needs work before the first send.
