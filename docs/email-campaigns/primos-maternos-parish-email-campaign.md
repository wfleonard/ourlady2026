# Primos Maternos Parish Email Campaign

## Our Lady of Guadalupe — Parish Outreach

This three-email sequence is designed for direct outreach from the Primos Maternos Google Business/Gmail account to Catholic parishes with an interest in Our Lady of Guadalupe.

The campaign emphasizes the documented provenance of the source image while carefully distinguishing the historical certifications from the modern canvas reproductions, which are printed in the United States.
