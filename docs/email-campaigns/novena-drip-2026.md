**Subject:** Day 1 of the Novena — the first apparition

**Preview text:** "Know that I am the Virgin Mary, Mother of the true God…"

**Body:**

Dear {{FirstName}},

**Day 1 · December 3, 1531 at Tepeyac**

On that Saturday morning, Mary spoke to Juan Diego for the first time:

> *"My dear son, whom I love tenderly, know that I am the Virgin Mary, Mother of the true God, Giver and Maintainer of life, Creator of all things, Lord of heaven and earth, Who is in all places. I wish a temple to be erected here where I can manifest the compassion I have for the natives and for all who solicit my help."*

Your words, heavenly Mother, fill my heart with love and gratitude. I turn to you today confident of obtaining through your intercession the grace which will enable me to live in accordance with the teaching of your Divine Son. Do not abandon me in this vale of tears. Have compassion on my anxieties and cares.

Pray four Hail Marys — one for each apparition — and the Memorare.

## Dec 6 — Day 4 · The image as written message

**Subject:** Day 4 — the image itself preaches

**Preview text:** Every detail of the tilma told the Aztecs something specific.

**Body:**

Dear {{FirstName}},

**Day 4 · The image as picture writing**

To an Aztec looking at the tilma in 1531, the image was a codex. Your regal figure in human form told them that your dwelling place had once been on earth. The rays of the sun, the clouds around your body, the stars on your garment, the crescent under your feet, the angel — all of them said: my home is now in heaven, and the human soul is immortal.

O Holy Mary, let the same image teach me never to forget the immortality of my soul, that Heaven is my goal and my inheritance. Amid the temptations of this life, let me think always of that home of peace and eternal bliss.

**Day 7 · The cross of our redemption**

At her throat, the tilma bears a small black cross on a gold brooch. It was the same cross the Aztecs had seen on the banner of Hernando Cortés. When they saw it on her image, they went to the Spanish missionaries eager to know its meaning — and were told of the Savior who came from heaven to redeem the human race, and of the Virgin Mother of that Savior.

Holy Virgin, Mary of Guadalupe, my Queen and Mother, inspire us to see in this miraculous picture the instrument of conversion, you who are the adorable Queen and Mother of the Apostles and Missionaries.

## Dec 10 — Day 8 · The roses at Tepeyac

**Subject:** Day 8 — "You are my ambassador worthy of confidence"

**Preview text:** The last words Juan Diego heard from her lips.

**Body:**

Dear {{FirstName}},

**Day 8 · The roses**

Juan Diego cut the roses blooming on the frozen hilltop and brought them down. Mary arranged them in his tilma with her own hands and tied it around his neck. The last words he heard from her:

> *"You are my ambassador worthy of confidence. Go in peace."*

The picture on that coarse cloth could not have been painted by human hands. It convinced the bishop, as it ought to convince every person who looks at it, that Juan Diego's message was truly hers.

Humbly, my Queen and Mother, let me be your ambassador like Juan Diego today — to give the doctrine contained in your Image to my friends, believers and unbelievers alike.

Four Hail Marys. The Memorare.

## Dec 11 — Day 9 · The conversion of a nation

**Subject:** Day 9 — nine million baptisms in ten years

**Preview text:** The last day of the Novena, the day before the feast.

**Body:**

Dear {{FirstName}},

**Day 9 · The conversion of a nation**

O Holy Virgin, enthroned on the spot you chose in the very heart of an idolatrous nation, you worked its conversion. After seeing the Image on the tilma of their countryman, the Indians stripped their temples of idols. They built churches on whose towers gleaming crosses shone in the sun. The huge drum that had innumerable times announced human sacrifice was heard no more.

As Juan Diego spent the remaining seventeen years of his life giving your spoken message to the Indians, so permit me, my loving Mother, to help in making known your celestial message among my countrymen. Animate and bless all who join in the Apostleship and Novena, having as their banner your admirable image on Juan Diego's tilma, together with the cross, emblem of our redemption.
