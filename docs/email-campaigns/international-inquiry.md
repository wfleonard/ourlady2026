# International Inquiry — Reply

For anyone outside the United States who asks about buying. Checkout on primosmaternos.com ships to the 50 states and Puerto Rico only; the same canvases are on eBay, whose international shipping delivers abroad and handles customs. In the mailer, give the contact segment `International` and this email is suggested for them.

The eBay links match `EBAY_LISTINGS` in `app/src/lib/site.ts`. The 12×18 gallery wrap has no eBay listing yet, so it is not offered here.
