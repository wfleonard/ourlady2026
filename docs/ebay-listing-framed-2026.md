## eBay category recommendations

**Primary — most-visited by buyers actively searching for religious art:**

> **Collectibles > Religion & Spirituality > Christianity > Other Christian Collectibles**
> Category ID **1799** (varies slightly by eBay's live tree — take whatever eBay auto-suggests when you type "Christian" in the category picker)

This is where buyers looking for a Guadalupe canvas for a parish, home altar, or gift actually browse. Higher intent than general art buyers.

**Secondary (list in a second category if you're on eBay Store plan and have the slot):**

> **Art > Art Prints**
> Category ID **360**

Broader audience — art collectors sometimes want religious pieces, and this category has ~10× the traffic. Downside: harder to stand out.

**Also worth trying if your listing performance is soft after 30 days:**

> **Home & Garden > Home Décor > Posters, Prints & Paintings > Prints > Wall Décor**

Gift-buyer intent (people shopping for someone's home). Less competition than pure art, less religious-specific matching than the Christianity category.

**What to avoid:**
- ✗ *Fine Art > Paintings* — this is a **print**, not an original painting. Wrong category will get delisted or flagged.
- ✗ *Antiques > Religious* — the frame and canvas are contemporary; the image is a reproduction.

## What changed from a typical old eBay listing

Same corrections applied on the site and in the [stretched-canvas eBay doc](ebay-listing-2026.md):

- **No** "the only replica authorized by the Catholic Church in existence" — not what the 1998 document establishes
- **No** Pope Francis blessing — undocumented; only Pope John Paul II's Jan 25, 1999 blessing of the Virgen Peregrina is
- **Two certificates from Mexico**, not a vague "certificate of authenticity"
- Provenance separated: **image created in Mexico**, **canvas printed in the USA**
- Substance replaces marketing filler: Kuhn pigment analysis, 1979 infrared, nitric acid + 1921 bomb
