Outreach to Catholic schools named for Our Lady of Guadalupe, the week before the Jubilee opens on October 12. The offers match the schools email sequence (`email-campaigns/segment-c-catholic-schools.md`), and the claims match the authenticity notes. Links carry `utm_campaign=linkedin-schools-2026`.

## Who to find

In LinkedIn search (or Sales Navigator), search **"Our Lady of Guadalupe School"**, **"Our Lady of Guadalupe Catholic School"** and **"Our Lady of Guadalupe Academy"**, then filter by current title. In order of fit:

1. Principal or head of school (decides on bulk orders)
2. Religion teacher or religion department chair
3. Campus minister

Aim for about 60 connection requests this week, 15 a day Monday to Thursday. That stays well inside LinkedIn's weekly invitation limit and leaves room for replies. Track who you contacted in the mailer: add each school contact with segment `C` so the schools email sequence is ready if they share an email address.
