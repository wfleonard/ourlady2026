# Image Fidelity — Our Lady of Guadalupe

## What the Historical Certification Establishes

The strongest documentary statement concerning the fidelity of the image associated with Primos Maternos appears in a Mexican certification dated **December 12, 1998** and signed by **Cardinal Norberto Rivera Carrera, Archbishop Primate of Mexico**.

The certification states:

> **“Certifico que la presente impresión es una reproducción fiel del archivo digital del Sagrado Original de la Imagen de Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe.”**

### English Translation

> **“I certify that this print is a faithful reproduction of the digital archive of the Sacred Original of the Image of Our Lady of Guadalupe.”**

The same document identifies:

> **“Captura del archivo digital y reproducción: DIGITAL COLOR S.A. DE C.V.”**

### English Translation

> **“Digital archive capture and reproduction: DIGITAL COLOR S.A. DE C.V.”**

---

## Why the Words “Faithful Reproduction” Matter

The Spanish phrase **“reproducción fiel”** is important.

The certification does not merely identify the subject as an image of Our Lady of Guadalupe. Cardinal Rivera specifically certifies the associated print as a **faithful reproduction of the digital archive of the Sacred Original**.

This provides documentary support for the fidelity of that certified reproduction to the digital archive identified in the certificate.

It does **not**, by itself, establish that every later reproduction made from a related digital image has been individually examined or certified by Cardinal Rivera or the Archdiocese.

## What We Can State With Confidence

Based on the surviving December 12, 1998 certification:

- A **digital archive of the Sacred Original Image of Our Lady of Guadalupe** existed by December 12, 1998.
- The certification identifies **Digital Color, S.A. de C.V.** with the digital archive capture and reproduction.
- **Cardinal Norberto Rivera Carrera** certified the associated print as a **“reproducción fiel” — “faithful reproduction” — of that digital archive**.
- The certification provides contemporaneous documentary evidence connecting the associated reproduction to a digital archive explicitly identified as being of the **Sacred Original**.

---

## What We Do Not Claim

Primos Maternos came under its current ownership in **2005**, several years after the 1998 certification.

The surviving documentation does not allow us to independently establish every technical or custodial step that occurred before current ownership.

Accordingly, we do **not** claim that:

- Cardinal Rivera individually certified each canvas sold by Primos Maternos today.
- Pope Saint John Paul II individually blessed today's Primos Maternos canvases.
- The Archdiocese of Mexico or Basilica currently endorses Primos Maternos or its products.
- The precise date of the original digital capture is known from the surviving certification.
- A surviving primary document in our possession identifies the person who specifically authorized Digital Color, S.A. de C.V. to perform the original digital capture.
- The historical certification, standing alone, proves every intermediate digital-file transfer between the certified 1998 reproduction and the digital file used for current production.

These distinctions are important to maintaining an accurate historical record.

## What “Fidelity” Means for Primos Maternos

When Primos Maternos discusses the **fidelity** of the historical image, the claim is grounded in the language of the December 12, 1998 certification.

The documentary claim is:
